iPhone Security Run Sheet

Official device settings only. Do not install “hacker remover” apps from a YouTube description.

0 — Decide

Run the full list only if you have two or more: unknown Apple ID devices, apps you did not install, login emails you did not request, surprise charges, or heat plus battery drain with no heavy use.

1 — Lock the door

  1. Airplane Mode. Turn Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth off if they stay on.
  2. Do not tap links until you finish.
  3. Change passwords from a second trusted device if you have one.

2 — Apple ID

  1. Settings → your name → Sign-In & Security. Two-factor On.
  2. Settings → your name → Devices. Remove anything that is not yours.
  3. If a stranger is listed, change the Apple ID password now. Prefer appleid.apple.com on another device.

3 — Passwords that matter

  1. Apple ID, primary email, bank apps, authenticator backup.
  2. Two-factor on. Prefer an authenticator app over SMS when you can.
  3. Settings → Passwords. Delete logins you do not use.

4 — Apps and battery

  1. Settings → General → iPhone Storage. Delete what you did not install.
  2. Settings → Battery. Note unknown high-background apps.

5 — System

  1. Software Update.
  2. Strong device passcode.
  3. Find My iPhone On.
  4. Lockdown Mode only if you are a high-risk target.

6 — Money

Check bank and card apps. Call the number on the card, not a number from a text.

7 — Erase last

Settings → General → Transfer or Reset iPhone → Erase All Content and Settings. Set up as new. Reinstall from the App Store one batch at a time.

Do not

  • Install antivirus from a YouTube QR or description.
  • Send passcodes or 2FA codes to anyone who messaged you first.
  • Pay a stranger to “remove the hacker.”
